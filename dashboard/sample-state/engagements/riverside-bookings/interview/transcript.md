## Core module

**Agent:** Before we begin — can you confirm you're the owner of Riverside
Bookings with authority to sign off the manifesto we produce today?

**Owner:** Yes, sole owner.

**Agent:** In one sentence: what does Riverside Bookings do, and for whom?

**Owner:** We run riverboat trips and event hire on the river — tourists in
summer, corporate bookings year-round.

**Agent:** What would make the next 12 months a success — a number, not a
feeling?

**Owner:** Honestly? 30% more direct bookings so we're not paying commission
on everything.

**Agent:** Recording that as the North Star candidate: *direct bookings +30%
in 12 months, baseline to be confirmed from your booking system export.* I'm
marking the baseline **TBC** against you as owner — I won't invent a number.
You mentioned commission — who takes it today?

**Owner:** Two agency platforms. Around 18% blended, I think. I can send the
statements.

**Agent:** Please do — I'll store them verbatim in the evidence locker and
cite them rather than assuming the 18%. *(→ locker item LK-002 received
later this session.)*
